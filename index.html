I work with traditional and independent music, decolonial approaches to culture, and contemporary cultural infrastructures.

Bulat (Kazbulat) Khalilov is an ethnographer, researcher, journalist and curator from Nalchik, and co-founder of Ored Recordings.

He is currently a Guest Researcher at the Institute of Cultural Anthropology / European Ethnology (KAEE), University of Göttingen.

His research is supported by the Right Livelihood Foundation.

About

My work develops across research, music, journalism and curatorial practice.

I am interested in traditional and post-traditional music, decoloniality, identity, archives, cultural mediation, and the ways in which music circulates through contemporary scenes and institutions.

A central part of my work is Ored Recordings , an independent label and platform documenting, studying, publishing and promoting traditional and local music of the North Caucasus.
